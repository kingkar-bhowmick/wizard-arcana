# How to play Wizard Arcana

Win exactly as many tricks as you bid. A **trick** is one card from each player, played in turn. One person wins each trick. The match winner is the player with the most points after the final round. Players on the same top score share the win.

## Choose a table

| Mode | Who plays |
| --- | --- |
| Solo practice | You and two to five bots |
| Online multiplayer | Three to six players on separate devices |
| Pass & play | Three to six players sharing a device |

**Classic** lasts 20 rounds with three players, 15 with four, 12 with five, or 10 with six. **Quick** lasts six rounds at any table size.

## 1. Deal and check trump

The deck has 60 cards. Blue, Green, Red, and Yellow each have ranks 1–13. Four Wizards and four Jesters complete the deck.

Everyone gets one card in round one, two in round two, and so on. The dealer rotates each round. When there is a card left to turn up, a numbered card sets its suit as trump, a Wizard lets the dealer pick a suit, and a Jester means no trump. When the deal uses the whole deck, there is no trump.

## 2. Bid your tricks

Starting with the player after the dealer, each person bids from zero to the number of tricks in the round. For example, in a three-card round you can bid 0, 1, 2, or 3. Select your bid and confirm it.

When **plus/minus bidding** is enabled, the final bidder cannot bring the sum of the bids to the exact number of tricks in the round. If there are three tricks and the earlier bids total two, the last player cannot bid one.

## 3. Play your cards

The player after the dealer leads the first trick. Read the turn prompt, select a card, then choose **Play card**.

- When a numbered suit has been led, play a card of that suit if you have one, or play a Wizard or Jester.
- If you do not hold the led suit, you may play any card.
- If a Jester leads, the first numbered card sets the suit unless a Wizard comes first. A Wizard played before any numbered card leaves the suit open.
- The first Wizard played wins. With no Wizard, the highest trump wins, followed by the highest card of the led suit if nobody played trump. If every card is a Jester, the first Jester wins.
- The winner leads the next trick.

For example, with Blue as trump, a Blue 2 beats a Red 13 even when Red was led. A Wizard played in that trick beats both cards.

## 4. Score the round

| Bid | Tricks won | Points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 0 | +20 |
| 2 | 2 | +40 |
| 2 | 1 | −10 |
| 1 | 3 | −20 |

An exact bid earns **20 + 10 × tricks won**. A missed bid loses **10 × the difference**. Open the round scorecard during the match to see bids, tricks won, and points earned in each completed round.
